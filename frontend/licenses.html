How CreatorProof Licensing Works

Your license selection becomes part of your content registration information.

1

Upload Content

Select your original digital file and provide its content details.

2

Select License

Choose the license type that defines how your content may be used.

3

Generate Fingerprint

CreatorProof generates a unique SHA-256 fingerprint for the file.

4

Register on Blockchain

The ownership proof is registered through the CreatorProof smart contract.

✓

License information stays connected to your ownership proof

CreatorProof uses SHA-256 fingerprinting together with Ethereum Sepolia blockchain registration to create a verifiable record for your registered content.
